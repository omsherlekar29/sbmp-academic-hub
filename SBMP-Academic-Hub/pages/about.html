About this hub

SBMP Academic Hub is a student-built academic portal for the Computer Engineering Diploma, Semester I, Division B at Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic. It puts the timetable, attendance, syllabus, people, notices and planning that first-year students otherwise chase across PDFs and group chats into one place.

What's inside

Timetable
Weekly Division B schedule with S1/S2 lab batches, current-period highlighting and a print layout.
Attendance
Import the official college report to track theory, tutorials and practicals against the 75% threshold, with a what-if simulator.
Syllabus
Unit-wise portion, course outcomes, practicals and study resources for all seven Semester I subjects.
People
Faculty directory and the Division B student list.
Notices & calendar
Department notices plus the academic calendar, downloadable as a .ics file.
Planning
A task planner for every subject, and a feedback form for improvements.

Built to be private

The portal is a static site: there is no server, no account and no analytics. Attendance numbers, planner tasks and feedback stay in your own browser's local storage and are never uploaded. The only external request is the PDF.js library loaded from a CDN when you choose to import a report.

The student list intentionally stores only roll number, salutation, name and batch — no SAP or registration numbers.
