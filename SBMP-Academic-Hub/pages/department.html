Overview

The Computer Engineering Department offers the Diploma in Computer Engineering programme, preparing students for industry and further engineering study through a curriculum that combines foundational science, mathematics, computing systems, and applied practice.

Semester I grounds First-Year students in mathematics, applied science, communication skills, engineering graphics, computing fundamentals, human values, and web technologies.

About this hub

SBMP Academic Hub is a student-built academic portal for the Computer Engineering Diploma, Semester I, Division B, at Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic. It gathers the information a first-year student reaches for daily into one place instead of scattered PDFs and group chats.

The portal brings together the division timetable with batch filters, the unit-wise syllabus and portion, subject resources, the faculty and student directories, notices, a task planner, and an attendance tracker that reads the official college report and projects the mandatory 75% threshold. Everything runs in the browser: the attendance numbers you import or type are stored on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.
